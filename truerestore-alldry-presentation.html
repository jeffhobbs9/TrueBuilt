The two moves that de-risk the whole thing

How I'd actually land them

Move 1 · kill the switching risk

Coexist, don't rip-and-replace

Don't ask them to drop Encircle. Keep it and run TrueRestore on 3–4 live claims in parallel — a short, comped trial, so no double-paying. The owner feels the higher approved ticket at zero risk, then drops Encircle at switch (that's when the software-wash kicks in). Trial, not migration.

Move 2 · highest-leverage entry

Beachhead = multi-unit owners

Land the owners who run 4+ territories first. One sale = several locations and a natural Franchise Advisory Council voice. Density and credibility in a single conversation — the fastest path to the tipping point.

Low-risk entry × high-leverage targets = the tipping point comes faster.
These two moves are what turn a good land-and-expand into a sharp one. Coexist-then-convert is the single biggest de-risker — the whole deal hinges on getting off Encircle, and "run it in parallel on a few real claims" removes the fear entirely. Multi-unit beachhead is leverage: one owner with 4 locations is 4 conversions plus an FAC voice.
